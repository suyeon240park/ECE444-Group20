# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We, the members of Team Nexus (ECE444 Group 20), pledge to make working on *What's in My Food?* a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy team.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment includes:

- showing empathy and kindness toward teammates;
- being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, in code review and in requirements review;
- accepting responsibility for our mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from the experience;
- communicating early when we will miss a deadline or are blocked;
- respecting each other's time, including agreed meeting times and the 24-hour response expectation;
- focusing on what is best for the whole team and for the people who will use the application.

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission;
- taking credit for a teammate's work, or misrepresenting who contributed what;
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or academic setting.

## Academic integrity

This is a University of Toronto course project. All members follow the University's Code of Behaviour on Academic Matters. Generative AI may be used where the course permits it; whoever submits AI-assisted work remains responsible for checking it.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all team spaces: this repository, its issues and pull requests, the project board, the team Discord server, meetings, and any time a member represents the team, such as in presentations or contact with users and the teaching team.

## Responsibilities

Every team member is responsible for following these standards and for speaking up, respectfully, when they see them broken. The Project Manager is the first point of contact for conduct concerns. If the concern involves the Project Manager, contact the Software Lead instead.

## Reporting and enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to:

- Eyad Ahmed, Project Manager: eyad.ahmed@mail.utoronto.ca
- Frank Liu, Software Lead: frankqt.liu@mail.utoronto.ca

Reports are handled promptly, fairly and with respect for the privacy of everyone involved. Nobody will be treated worse for making a report in good faith.

Concerns are resolved through the process in Section 4 of our [Team Workflow Document](Team_Workflow_Document.md):

1. **Direct conversation.** The people involved discuss the issue openly and try to resolve it.
2. **Team discussion.** If that does not resolve it, the full team discusses it and agrees on corrective steps, which are recorded in the meeting minutes.
3. **Escalation.** If the behaviour continues or is serious, the team documents it and brings it to the ECE444 teaching team.

Serious misconduct, including harassment or discrimination, may be reported directly to the teaching team or to the appropriate University of Toronto office without going through the earlier steps.
